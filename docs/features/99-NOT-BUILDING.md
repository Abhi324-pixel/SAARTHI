# Things in TESSA and Chhatrachhaya that SAARTHI deliberately does **not** build

Writing this down stops people from adding them "because the source has it". Each item says why not and what SAARTHI does instead. Revisit an item only by a team decision, and
re-read the safeguarding rules in `00-PLAYBOOK.md` §4 first.

| # | Item (where it is) | Why we are not building it | What SAARTHI does instead |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Community feed** — Chhatrachhaya "Gyan Ki Baat": `src/FeedScreen.tsx`, `src/pages/FeedPage.tsx`, Supabase `posts` table (`supabase/migrations/20240422000000_init_tables.sql`, which also does `disable row level security`) | An open social feed with minors needs 24×7 moderation, reporting and legal review. It is off-mission (SAARTHI measures learning and routes problems to a mentor). The migration shows the trap: RLS turned off. | Case-based mentor chat only (inside a case, screened, logged). Cohort view for teachers. |

Mentor sessions stay text-in-app (C02). |
| 3 | **Presence / "N people online" counters** — `src/App.tsx` l.244–284 (Firebase Realtime DB `onDisconnect`) | Reveals who is online (privacy risk for minors and mentors) and adds a second realtime system. | Nothing. Polling for case updates only (C04). |

## When might we revisit?

- **Community / voice rooms:** only after a safeguarding policy, moderation staffing and legal review exist — and as a separate product decision.
- **Camera coaching:** not planned. If ever, opt-in, adult-only, on-device processing, never used for any score or matching.
- **E-mail beyond guardian consent:** only with explicit opt-in and an unsubscribe path; never to minors without the D01 flow.
